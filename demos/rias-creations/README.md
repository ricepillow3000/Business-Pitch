# Ria's Creations Salon — website demo

A one-page website mock-up to show Ria (Lilburn, GA). No install needed.

**To open it:** double-click `index.html`. It opens in your web browser.
(Fonts and the map need internet; everything else works offline.)

## What's on the page

1. **Hero** — salon photo, "Book" and "Call" buttons, 4.5★ Google rating card
2. **Specials ribbon** — $35 Brazilian wax, $65 full-body wax (from her posters)
3. **About** — arched photos (her mirrors are arches)
4. **Services menu** — tabs: Hair · Brows & Face · Facials · Wax & Massage
5. **Gallery** — her two photos
6. **Reviews** — three real Google quotes
7. **Book a visit** — request form with time slots (demo only: nothing is sent)
8. **Visit** — address, phone, hours, Google map, directions button

On phones a floating **Call / Book** bar appears after you scroll.
