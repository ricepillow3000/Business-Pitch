Privacy

Draft for Ria to review before the site goes live. It is plain-language, not legal advice.

Last updated November 2026.

What we collect

When you request a visit, we ask for your first name, mobile number, the service, the day and time you prefer, and any notes you choose to add.

How we use it

  • To confirm your visit and send you a reminder the day before, by text.
  • To let Ria prepare for your service.

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Text messages

We only text you about visits you asked for. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop texts, or HELP for help.

How long we keep it

We keep booking details for as long as you are a client, so we can remember your preferences. Ask us and we'll delete them.

Cookies and storage

This site remembers your day or evening setting on your own device. It doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. The map on the page is provided by Google, which has its own privacy policy.
