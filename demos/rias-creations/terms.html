Booking requests

Sending a request online doesn't book the time on its own. Your visit is confirmed once you enter the code we text you, or when the salon confirms by phone or text.

Changes and cancellations

Plans change. Reply to your reminder text or call the salon as early as you can so the time can go to someone else.

Prices

Prices on the site are starting prices. Length, thickness and the products used can change the final price, and Ria will always tell you before she starts. Specials run for a limited time.

Walk-ins

Walk-ins are welcome when there is an open chair. If you would like Ria specifically, call ahead.

Payment

We accept cards, checks and tap to pay.
